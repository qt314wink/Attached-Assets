# IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS — STYLE FORWARD

## Visual Language Guidelines

**Color palette — vibrant but intentional:**

- Primary: Electric violet (#7c3aed), hot coral (#ff6b6b), luminous cyan (#22d3ee)
- Secondary: Deep ink (#0f172a), warm cream (#fef3c7), acid green (#a3e635)
- Accent: Gold (#fbbf24), rose (#fb7185), electric blue (#3b82f6)
- Use bold color blocking, not gradients — flat color with depth from overlap

**Style references:**

- Memphis design meets data visualization
- Bauhaus geometry with contemporary color
- Risograph texture and overprint effects
- Editorial illustration for tech publications (The Verge, Wired, MIT Tech Review aesthetic)
- NOT: Corporate minimalism, stock vector, generic tech imagery

**Mood:** Confident, distinctive, intellectually playful, visually memorable
